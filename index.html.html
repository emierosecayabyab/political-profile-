Personal Background

A brief biographical overview

Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr., commonly known as “Bongbong” Marcos or “BBM,” was born on September 13, 1957, in Batac, Ilocos Norte. He is the son of former President Ferdinand E. Marcos Sr. and former First Lady Imelda Romualdez Marcos. He became the 17th President of the Philippines after the May 9, 2022 national election.

Political Career

Selected government positions

1981–1983
Vice Governor, Ilocos Norte

Entered provincial government service.

1983–1986
Governor, Ilocos Norte

Served as provincial governor.

1992–1995
Representative, 2nd District of Ilocos Norte

Served in the House of Representatives.

1998–2007
Governor, Ilocos Norte

Returned to provincial leadership.

2007–2010
Representative, 2nd District of Ilocos Norte

Returned to the House of Representatives.

2010–2016
Senator of the Philippines

Served in the 15th and 16th Congresses.

2022–present
President of the Philippines

Assumed office on June 30, 2022.
